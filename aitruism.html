A four-layer defence model

Each layer answers a different question about what your AI is doing — and every one reports to a person, not just a dashboard.

LAYER 1

Discovery & Policy

Declarative, dynamic policy enforcement — what the AI is allowed to do, decided in the open.

LAYER 2

Observability

Real-time usage monitoring and anomaly alerts, streaming to your SIEM or XDR.

LAYER 3

Behavioral Monitoring

Adaptive analysis for bias, drift and prompt-injection — catching what static rules miss.

LAYER 4

Grounding Validation

Checks AI output against its retrieved sources to prevent hallucination and contradiction.

Above every layer sits the one control that can't be automated: a human who is accountable.
